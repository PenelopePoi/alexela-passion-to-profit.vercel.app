AleXelA — Turn Your Passion into Profit

Turn a skill into a clear offer, a fair price, and a practical first step. Created by a working artist and entrepreneur who knows the gap between talent and a sustainable income.

1. Build an offer someone can understand

Choose one customer and one problem you can solve. Write: “I help [customer] get [result] through [deliverable].” Specify what is included, quantity, turnaround, revision limits, and what the customer supplies. Start with a manageable paid service rather than a promise to do everything.

2. Calculate before you quote

Add materials, production time, design time, overhead allocation, delivery, waste allowance, and transaction costs. Set a price that covers those costs and the margin your business needs. Profit margin and markup are different: price = cost ÷ (1 − desired margin). A $60 cost at a 40% margin gives a $100 price. This example is not a local market quote.

3. Present proof and find customers

Choose three representative samples, describe the result, and obtain permission for client work. Make a simple offer with a clear contact method. Start with people or businesses who need that result, track inquiries and follow-ups, and ask satisfied customers for permission to share their feedback.

4. Agree on the work and payment

Document scope, deliverables, timeline, revisions, approvals, deposit/payment terms, cancellation, and ownership or licensing. Research your registration, tax, and permit obligations using official sources, and get qualified advice for your circumstances. Keep invoices and expense records.

Official starting points: Florida Sunbiz, IRS small-business resources, U.S. Copyright Office.

5. Connect the brand to production

Use AleXelA’s design and production services for your logo, website, cards, flyers, apparel, decals, and signs. Before approval, check spelling, contact information, intended dimensions, safe zones, image resolution, color layers, vector paths, and final QR decoding. Cut vinyl needs solid-color editable paths; heat-transfer artwork needs the correct mirrored version. Preserve approved assets and versions.
