# AleXelA Creative - Mandatory Client Production Standard

Effective 2026-10-13. These instructions take precedence over older references to XELA / Ali-Xiel / Ali-X. Client-facing studio name: **AleXelA Creative - Passion to Profit**.

## Exact sequence for EVERY new client
1. Research comparable local and online designers and print suppliers. Evaluate current market prices, quality, turnaround, material costs, margins, and professional presentation. Never represent estimates as verified quotes.
2. Create or upgrade the master logo FIRST. If a logo is supplied, preserve exact geometry, orientation, colors, emblem, and all client-approved details. Do not redraw or distort the master. Obtain approval before derivative layouts.
3. Confirm and record business name, owner/role where applicable, phone, email, website, socials, services, service areas, slogans, brand colors, licensing/insurance/certifications and relevant registration facts. Ask only for missing necessary information. **Never invent verification, certifications, insurance or license status.**
4. Produce matching business card and flyer from the exact approved logo; optimize for legible contacts, high-contrast hierarchy, service clarity and conversion. Produce each as two proofs: visible repeated AleXelA watermark over the entire proof AND watermark-free clean copy. Keep the same background/style unless asked to change it.
5. Create BLACK and WHITE shirt mockups. Provide FRONT and BACK for both colors, with two versions each: a repeated watermark fully covering every shirt for client proofing and a completely clean, watermark-free copy. FRONT: small straight emblem ONLY, no business words unless explicitly requested. BACK: full approved logo, phone, slogan, services, service areas/credentials when applicable, all inside shirt printable fabric. Optional gray mockup.
6. Generate real, accurately measured, watermark-free physical production artwork separate from mockups. Provide US Letter 10-per-page business cards (3.5 x 2 in), 4-per-page quarter-sheet flyers (4 x 5.25 in), and shirt heat-transfer pages with sizes explicitly marked. Supply trim marks, bleed/safe zones appropriate to the chosen method; no clipping or overlap.
7. For LARGE adult shirt-back transfer art, NEVER shrink to a single Letter sheet. Default completed back art is 12 x 15 inches at 300 DPI. TILE across FOUR US Letter sheets, numbered 1 top-left, 2 top-right, 3 bottom-left, 4 bottom-right, with 0.5-inch registration overlap and assembly guide. If a requested size is larger, compute the needed grid and output ALL numbered sheets. Check print margins with 100% actual-size scaling. White/light conventional sublimation: MIRRORED. The user's dark-fabric transfer paper for black shirts: NOT MIRRORED. Manufacturer compatibility, heat, pressure and peeling rules must be verified. Highlight that tiled transfers can leave visible seams; use a wide-format printer for truly seamless graphics.
8. Include layered or high-resolution transparent 300-DPI PNG master artwork, normal and mirrored as applicable, plus press/cut instructions. Package individual files and a ZIP for client delivery. Never watermark actual transfers.
9. Prepare one itemized customer quote: **design/setup fee charged ONCE for the entire requested package**; list separate material and production charges for cards, flyers, shirts, optional lamination, tax/shipping if applicable. Keep quote pricing consistent across chats, website, PDF, and follow-ups. NEVER add duplicate design fees per product. Update your rate card explicitly when prices change.
10. Before delivery: inspect every output at actual size; check spelling, phone, correct front/back, all sheet counts, correct logos (no tilt), no design off shirt, no text clipped, watermarks covering all client proofs and absent from clean print assets, accessibility/legibility, and quote arithmetic. Confirm filenames and links resolve. Research comparable businesses for premium quality and fair pricing.
